## Abstract

A simulated 10K race with eleven training platforms named, one thousand synthetic runners each and a confirmation at ten thousand per platform: the mapping from the original study design to the current simulator, results per platform on both the raw and the normalized QDOT scale, analysis, limitations and the scale-up.

The simulator white paper reports this field by delivery class; this report names the platforms.
