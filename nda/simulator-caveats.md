## Abstract

Known limitations, simplifications and modeling gaps in the training simulator, each classified by severity — how much it could affect conclusions — and by tractability — how feasible a fix is. It covers the session-level simplification, asymmetries in the spacing penalty, volume bias in earlier models, session-shortening mechanics, calibration gaps, the readiness proxy, divergence between deterministic and cohort runs, how faithfully external protocols are represented, and the optimization asymmetry.

It is maintained independently of the white paper and the mathematical specification.
