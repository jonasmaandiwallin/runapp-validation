## Abstract

Every known limitation of the QDOT instrument at epoch v0.4, each classified as either unresolvable with the data available or resolvable but not yet done, and each with the measurement or analysis that grounds the assessment. It opens with the disclosures every published QDOT ranking must carry, then covers parameters whose value is not validated, the model's structural assumptions, measurement and coverage gaps, open citations, what is deliberately not a caveat, and the review the instrument has passed.

The QDOT white paper names three public caveats; this register holds all of them.
